# Multiple-rest discovery and registration plans (WIP)

multiple_rest_detection connects native admission to serif detection, collects successful plans from SystemCandidates in candidate registration order, retains last LEFT and first RIGHT peaks, and records rest grade/median/height/staff plus LEFT/RIGHT serif geometry and impacts. It always searches both sides even if LEFT is empty, matching original getSerifPeaks. All plans are discovered before any registration. No beam/group/registry mutation is implemented in this stage; those structures therefore retain their current contents.

Eight focused tests pass. The inherited independent original 75 synthetic side controls (42 accepted peaks in 41 positive cases and 34 negative cases) are additionally fed through admission/discovery and the witnessed side matches exactly. Opposite-side native searches have no complete-rest original witness. New selection/order and registration geometry tests are native contract tests based on pinned bytecode; they are NOT original complete-rest conversion controls. The original oracle fixture and observer remain unchanged.

Pinned createInters builds serif filaments from vLag then hLag sections, stable-sorts by abscissa, registers original glyphs, creates rest then LEFT/RIGHT serif inters and relations, then process deletes all converted beams only after all creation. AbstractInter.remove(true) handles last-member ensemble removal and graph incident-edge deletion. BarFilamentFactory core selection and iterative section attachment were traced and the traces are preserved. This WIP does not yet implement or verify those mutation steps.
